What do plants produce during photosynthesis?
📝 AI summary
Key concepts at a glance
Photosynthesis is a biological process in plants, algae and some bacteria that converts light energy into chemical energy stored as glucose. It takes place in chloroplasts — the thylakoid membranes handle light-dependent reactions, while the Calvin cycle in the stroma handles light-independent reactions. Key inputs: CO₂, H₂O, and light. Key outputs: glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) and oxygen. For your exam, focus on the two stages, the organelle location, and the overall equation.
